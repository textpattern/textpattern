# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We welcome reports from security researchers and organisations. Before proceeding, please read and understand the following:

  https://textpattern.com/weblog/security-considerations-and-user-privileges-in-textpattern

We follow Full Disclosure Policy (RFPolicy) v2.0, and expect you to in return. The policy can be found here:

  https://wiretrip.net/rfp/policy.html

Please ensure that you have considered the following security precautions prior to reporting a security issue:

* Ensure any vulnerability or issue is not due to a third party script, malfunctioning server, or insufficient security precautions.
* Report any and all security vulnerabilities to us first. Do not publicly disclose information about potential security bugs.

Please report vulnerabilities to:

  security@textpattern.com

A member of the team will triage your report. Please provide vulnerability details and steps to replicate it.

Allow a reasonable amount of time for us to assess and investigate your report, along with time to correct the issue before sharing details publicly.

We are a free, open-source project run by volunteers. We do not offer monetary rewards or provide ‘bug bounties’ for discovering and/or reporting security issues.

Security reports should be free of charge and voluntary on your part.

Thank you for your understanding.
